# Black and purple theme

2 November 2026. The owner explicitly requested black and purple as primary colors, purple gradients, and minimal secondary orange. This supersedes the previous copper palette and the research brief’s gradient/purple prohibition.

The canvas is near-black #09070D; surfaces use #0E0B14 and #15101F. Lavender #B69BFF carries readable accent text; purple gradients run from deep violet #140B20 through #361253 to #4B1D77. Buttons use #793ED2 to #502180 with white text. Orange #FF9A52 is limited to the name period and small role marker. Paper in the conceptual artwork is a cool lavender neutral. The actual TermWise screenshot is preserved.

The new palette applies to all static pages, original SVG artwork, the physical Three.js processor, fallback SVG, and generated social images. Motion and project priority remain intact. Principal text contrast is verified by scripts/check-contrast.py.

Validated six static groups, 104 core browser assertions, 60 spatial assertions, and nine contrast pairs. Inspected desktop, gradient-section and mobile captures under screenshots/theme/.
